# 🍊 Track Your Sales

### Your Sales. Your Success.

**Track Your Sales** is a modern sales performance tracking application designed to help Orange telecom employees monitor their targets, record achievements, and improve their daily performance.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Smart Dashboard** — Track your sales performance at a glance.
- 🎯 **Target Tracking** — Monitor targets and achievement percentages.
- 📱 **Sales Management** — Record new lines, Orange Cash wallets, Postpaid, DSL, points, and quality.
- 🍊 **Orangawy Mascot** — A friendly companion to keep you motivated.
- 📈 **Performance Reports** — Review your achievements and remaining targets.
- 💾 **Local Data Storage** — Save your records in your browser.
- 🔄 **Backup & Restore** — Export and import your data.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Designed for mobile phones and desktop screens.
- 🇪🇬 **Arabic Interface** — RTL layout with a modern Orange-inspired design.

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Browser Local Storage

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Set your employee name, branch, and monthly targets.
3. Record your daily achievements.
4. Monitor your progress from the dashboard.
5. Export a backup regularly to protect your records.

## 🔐 Privacy & Data

This version stores data locally in the browser on the device being used. It does not provide cloud synchronization or shared employee accounts.

**Important:** Clearing browser data may delete saved records. Export backups regularly.
